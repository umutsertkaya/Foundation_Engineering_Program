liq-mechanism

1 Before shaking
u ≈ hydrostatic
2 Shaking starts
u rising
3 u = σv
σ′ = σ − u → 0
The inter-granular contact stress (σ′) decreases by the pore pressure u.
When σ′ drops to zero the grains no longer carry each other and the soil behaves like a liquid.
Transparent tubes on the right (piezometers): the water level rises as u increases.
The opening of grain spacing is not a volume increase but a loss of contact (no drainage).
