Anchor levels

Assumptions
Apparent pressure envelope (sand), level ground: FHWA GEC-4 5.2.4–5.2.6 (sand and stiff clay trapezoid, figures 24 and 27; soft clay figure 23c). The method is empirical.
Anchor loads by the hinge method: hinges at every anchor except the first; bending moments from the same model.
Embedment: moment equilibrium about the toe under the base reaction R_B and the net pressure below the base (passive divided by the given factor of safety; where smaller, the passive pressure from tan φ′ and c′ divided by that factor is used, USACE EM 1110-2-2504 5-2d). This is deeper than the GEC-4 5.5.3 force criterion.
Embedment increase not given: D = D0 (1 + 0.2).
Anchor design inputs not given: anchor loads only, no free or bond length.
No steel or concrete section design: shear and moment demands are reported, M/W is compared with a given stress only.
Warnings
External (global) stability and deep-seated (Kranz) failure are not checked. Analyse slip surfaces passing behind and through the anchors separately by limit equilibrium; GEC-4 5.8.3 requires FS ≥ 1.3 (1.5 for critical permanent works).
Embedment comes from moment equilibrium about the toe (deeper than the GEC-4 5.5.3 force criterion, on the safe side). A toe force residual of 257.9 kN/m is not balanced and is not included in the shear maximum; assess it separately for the embedded section.
