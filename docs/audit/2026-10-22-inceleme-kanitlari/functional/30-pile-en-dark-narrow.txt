Assumptions

Layered Rankine active/passive pressure; separate front/retained profiles and groundwater; hydrostatic water; no seepage (Rankine (1857); P1 §1)
Passive pressure divided by FS=1.5; where smaller, the passive pressure from tan φ′ and c′ divided by FS is used (high φ′; USACE EM 1110-2-2504 5-2d) (USACE EM 1110-2-2504 Tablo 5-1: perde duvarı, serbest drenajlı zemin, olağan durum 1.50 (S durumu); ince daneli zeminde Q durumu 2.00 (programda ayrı değer yok))
Cantilever Blum embedment increased by 20%; free-earth support for anchored walls (Mevcut sheet_pile.py; USS Steel Sheet Piling Design Manual / USACE EM 1110-2-2504)
Sloping backfill β = 10°: Rankine slope coefficient (resultant parallel to the surface), front side horizontal; c′ ignored on sloping ground; seismic M-O (δ = β) stops when β > φ′ − ψ (Rankine (1857); Mononobe-Okabe; Das, Principles of Foundation Engineering Ch. 7)
Anchor load = wall force × spacing / cos(inclination); spacing 1 m, inclination 0° (SheetPileStructure; geotek.engine.sheet_pile.anchored_sheet_pile)
